# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Homes and businesses in equal measure: families who want an air conditioner installed or fixed at home, and local businesses (shops, offices, clinics, restaurants) that need their units working. They arrive from a phone, usually when it is hot or a unit has failed, and want a fast quote without a long process.

## Product Purpose

A one-page site that advertises Polar, an air-conditioning installation technician, and turns a visit into a WhatsApp quote request or a phone call. Success is a visitor sending a WhatsApp message or calling 352 156 3476.

## Positioning

A local technician in La Piedad, Michoacán and nearby towns who answers fast, quotes fairly with no surprise charges, and has years of experience in the trade. The customer deals with the technician directly.

## Operating Context

- Service area: La Piedad, Michoacán and surrounding municipalities.
- Services: installation, maintenance and repair of air conditioners (minisplit and window units), all brands, at the customer's home or business.
- Contact: phone and WhatsApp 352 156 3476 (wa.me/523521563476). Quotes are requested over WhatsApp with a prefilled message.
- Language: Spanish (Mexico).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JS site, no build step.
- Quote builder composes a WhatsApp message (service, unit type, optional name) and opens WhatsApp.
- Undecided: street address and map (pending from the owner), logo (pending), exact number of years of experience (not given; do not state a number).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Polar.
- Confirmed claims: "Garantía en cada instalación", "Servicio a domicilio", "Todas las marcas", fast response, fair prices, years of experience.
- Standing preference (chosen 2026-10-31): the category standard, played straight. The craft bar is the air-conditioning brand sites (Mirage, Carrier, Daikin): clean, trustworthy, large product photography.

## Evidence on Hand

- Phone/WhatsApp number (above).
- Hero photo: a stock wall-mounted split unit from Unsplash (`assets/img/hero-ac.jpg`), not Polar's own work.
- Owner will provide real photos of their installations later; until then imagery is placeholder and must not be presented as Polar's own work.
- No testimonials, reviews, customer counts, prices or certifications exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. The quote is one tap away from anywhere on the page.
2. Speak like a local tradesperson: plain Spanish, concrete, no corporate hype.
3. Only state what the owner confirmed.
4. Phone-first: most visitors are on a phone, often in the heat.
